What's inside each format

Procreate .brush

A ZIP holding Brush.archive, an Apple binary plist of the brush settings. Next to it sit Shape.png and Grain.png, where white means paint.

Clip Studio .sut

A SQLite database with one column per setting. Pressure curves are packed into binary "effector" blobs. Tips live in their own encrypted-header layer files.

Krita .bundle

A ZIP with a checksum manifest. Each preset is a PNG thumbnail with the settings hidden in a text chunk, next to its tips and textures.

The three apps paint with different brush engines, so no conversion is exact. Each converter lists the settings it couldn't carry over, so you know what to tweak.
