How it’s converted

each step explains what it does
01Read Presetspending
A Krita .bundle is a ZIP of presets, tips and patterns. Each .kpp preset is a PNG whose preset text chunk (often zlib-compressed) holds the settings as XML. Filter, clone and other engines that edit pixels instead of painting are skipped.
02Load Tips & Texturespending
Krita's generated tips (round, square, soft, gaussian) are drawn from their settings. Image tips are PNG or SVG files, and animated tips are GIMP .gih image hoses holding several images. Procreate paints with white, so every tip becomes a white-on-black Shape.png, and the texture becomes Grain.png.
03Map Settingspending
Krita drives each option with sensor curves. A pressure curve becomes Procreate's pressure amount, taken from where the curve starts, and a speed curve becomes speed size. A drawing-angle sensor on rotation becomes "follow stroke", and a random sensor becomes rotation scatter. Ratio becomes roundness and Scatter becomes jitter. Sizes are estimates, since Procreate doesn't document its size unit.
04Write Brushespending
Each brush gets a Brush.archive, an NSKeyedArchiver binary plist with a SilicaBrush object, written using the same key names Procreate uses. The thumbnails below are sample strokes drawn with the converted settings.
05Packagepending
A single brush becomes a .brush ZIP. Several become a .brushset: one folder per brush, named by its ID, and a brushset.plist listing them. Open either on your iPad (AirDrop or the Files app) to import it.
