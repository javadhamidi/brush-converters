How it’s converted

each step explains what it does
01Read Brushespending
A .brush is a ZIP. Its Brush.archive is an Apple binary plist written by NSKeyedArchiver: a flat $objects table whose SilicaBrush entry holds about 200 settings. A .brushset is a ZIP of brush folders listed in brushset.plist. The Reset/ copy some brushes carry is skipped.
02Decode Imagespending
Procreate paints with white: Shape.png is the tip and Grain.png the texture. Krita tips paint with dark ink and transparency, so the shape is turned into an alpha mask. Brushes that use one of Procreate's built-in shapes only store its name, so common ones (hard, soft, oval) are approximated.
03Map Parameterspending
Settings map onto Krita's Pixel brush:
  • Pressure and speed amounts become Pressure / Speed sensor curves.
  • Spacing maps directly, and jitter becomes Scatter.
  • Rotation follows the stroke or is random, and roundness becomes Ratio.
  • The grain becomes Krita's Texture option.
Brush sizes are estimates, since Procreate doesn't document its size unit. Wet mixing, taper and colour jitter have no Krita equivalent and are listed per brush.
04Write Presetspending
A .kpp preset is a PNG thumbnail with the preset XML in a text chunk. The thumbnails below are sample strokes drawn with the converted settings.
05Bundlepending
A Krita .bundle is a zip. It holds an uncompressed mimetype, a manifest with an MD5 for every file, the presets, the tips (brushes/) and the textures (patterns/). The presets are tagged with the bundle name.
